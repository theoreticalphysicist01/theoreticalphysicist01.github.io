Zeng-Zhao Li

Theoretical physicist working at the intersection of quantum information, open quantum systems, and non-Hermitian physics.

I am particularly interested in how dissipation, exceptional points, and structured environments can be used as resources for controlling quantum dynamics, transport, and information processing.

Research

My research develops theoretical tools for understanding and controlling quantum dynamics. I am particularly interested in how dissipation, exceptional points, and environmental structure can become useful resources—accelerating entanglement, directing energy and information transport, and improving the operation of solid-state quantum devices.
