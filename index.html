Research

My research explores how coherence, dissipation, and environmental structure shape quantum dynamics, and how these effects can be harnessed as resources for quantum technologies. Current interests center on non-Hermitian and exceptional-point physics, open-system dynamics, quantum information processing, and quantum transport.
